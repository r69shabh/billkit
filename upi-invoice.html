Make an invoice with a UPI QR code

Give clients a way to pay your INR invoice without typing your UPI ID or the amount. BillKit adds a scan-to-pay QR code directly to the invoice PDF.

Create a UPI invoice

How to add the QR code

  1. Open the invoice editor and set the currency to INR.
  2. Enter your invoice number, line items and tax if applicable. Add your receiving UPI ID under Payment.
  3. Check the preview for the QR code. Click Download PDF and choose Save as PDF in the print dialog.

What should I check before sending?

Scan the QR from the saved PDF in a UPI app. Check that it shows the right payee and the positive invoice total. You don't need to approve a payment to check these details.

Does BillKit collect the payment?

No. The QR code tells the payer's UPI app who to pay and includes the invoice number as a payment note. The payer approves the transfer in their app. Verify receipt with your bank or UPI app before marking the invoice paid.

Can I include GST too?

Yes. The free editor supports GSTIN, HSN/SAC, place of supply and the CGST/SGST or IGST split. See the GST invoice generator for the fields to check.
