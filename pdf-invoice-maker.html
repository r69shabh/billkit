Make a bill online and save it as a PDF

If you need to bill a client for your work or products, make an invoice: a payment request that shows the items, total and due date. BillKit is free to use without signing up. The free PDF has a small BillKit footer.

Make a bill with BillKit

From bill to PDF in four steps

  1. Say who is billing whom. Enter your business and client details, an invoice number, the issue date, and the agreed due date.
  2. Show what the client owes. List each item as quantity × rate. Choose the agreed currency, review discounts and other charges, and enter tax only if applicable.
  3. Tell them how to pay. Add your agreed payment instructions and any purchase order or payment reference the client needs.
  4. Save and send the PDF. Select Print / Save PDF. In the print dialog, pick Save as PDF or your device’s equivalent instead of a printer. Open the file, check the totals, then attach it to your email.

Fix common PDF problems

  • The last row or total is cut off: Try A4, portrait orientation, and default scaling. Check every page in preview; don’t print just page one if the invoice runs onto a second page.
  • The date, URL, or page title appears in the margins: If your print dialog has a Headers and footers setting, turn it off. That setting does not remove BillKit’s free-plan footer.
  • The dialog only shows printers: Look under Destination for a PDF option. On a phone, look for a PDF save or share action in the system print sheet, or open the editor in a desktop browser.

Give the PDF an identifiable name, for example INV-0042-Example-Client.pdf. Check that it opens and includes the client name, total, currency, due date and instructions before you attach it.

Invoice, bill, or receipt?

A client bill and an invoice can both request payment. A receipt confirms that payment was received; don’t send an unpaid invoice as proof of payment. This tool makes invoices for real sales or services, not third-party utility bills or proof-of-address documents.
