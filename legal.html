Privacy policy

BillKit has no user accounts and no backend database. The invoices, clients, logo and business details you enter are stored only in your browser's local storage on your device and are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes them, so export a backup if you need one.

When you activate Pro, your license key is sent directly from your browser to the payment provider's license API to check that it is valid. When you buy, the payment provider collects your name, email and payment details under its own privacy policy; we receive your email and purchase record from them so we can provide support and refunds.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Refund policy

If BillKit Pro is not right for you, email us within 14 days of purchase with your order email and we will refund you in full. Refunded licenses stop working.
