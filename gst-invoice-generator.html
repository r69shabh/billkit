What goes on a GST tax invoice

BillKit's invoice includes each of these fields. Until your GSTIN, state, place of supply and every item's HSN/SAC are filled in, it is titled "Invoice", not "Tax Invoice", and the editor lists what is missing.

  • Supplier name, address and GSTIN
  • A unique, consecutive invoice number and the invoice date
  • Recipient name, address and GSTIN (for registered buyers)
  • HSN code for goods or SAC code for services
  • Quantity, rate and taxable value of each item
  • GST rate and amount: CGST + SGST for supply within your state, IGST for supply to another state
  • Place of supply (state name and code), the total in figures and the total in words
  • Whether tax is payable on reverse charge, and an authorised signatory line

CGST + SGST or IGST?

BillKit compares your state (taken from your GSTIN) with the place of supply. If they match, it charges CGST + SGST and splits the rate in half (18% becomes 9% CGST and 9% SGST). If they differ, it charges the full rate as IGST. You can force either one, and the editor warns you when the override doesn't match the states. Shipping or other charges are taxed at your highest item rate unless you set another rate, since freight billed with a supply usually takes the GST of that supply. For exports of services, pick "Outside India" as the place of supply, set 0% and add your LUT details in the notes.

BillKit makes a normal PDF invoice. It does not generate an e-invoice (IRN/QR from the IRP), which businesses above the e-invoicing turnover limit must use. Check unusual cases with your CA.

Common SAC codes for freelancers

  • 998314: IT design and development services
  • 998313: IT consulting and support
  • 998361: Advertising services
  • 998391: Specialty design services (graphic, interior, fashion)
  • 998399: Other professional, technical and business services
  • 999293: Commercial training and coaching

Check the right code and rate for your service with your CA or the GST portal.
