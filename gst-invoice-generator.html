What goes on a GST tax invoice

BillKit's invoice includes each of these fields:

  • Supplier name, address and GSTIN
  • A unique, consecutive invoice number and the invoice date
  • Recipient name, address and GSTIN (for registered buyers)
  • HSN code for goods or SAC code for services
  • Quantity, rate and taxable value of each item
  • GST rate and amount: CGST + SGST for supply within your state, IGST for supply to another state
  • Place of supply, the total in figures and the total in words

CGST + SGST or IGST?

If your client is in the same state as you, choose CGST + SGST and BillKit splits the rate in half (18% becomes 9% CGST and 9% SGST). If your client is in a different state, choose IGST and the full rate is charged as IGST. For exports of services, use "No tax" or 0% and add your LUT details in the notes.

Common SAC codes for freelancers

  • 998314: IT design and development services
  • 998313: IT consulting and support
  • 998361: Advertising services
  • 998391: Specialty design services (graphic, interior, fashion)
  • 998399: Other professional, technical and business services
  • 999293: Commercial training and coaching

Check the right code and rate for your service with your CA or the GST portal.
