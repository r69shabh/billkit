Questions

Is the free version really free?

Yes. Make as many invoices as you want, with no signup and no trial timer. Pro is a one-time upgrade for branding and history.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser's local storage on your device. BillKit has no server that sees your invoices. Pro users can export a backup file at any time.

How do I get a PDF?

Click "Print / Save PDF" and choose "Save as PDF" in the dialog. On a phone, use Share → Save to Files or Print → Save as PDF.

Is it a valid GST tax invoice?

BillKit includes the fields a GST tax invoice needs: supplier and recipient GSTIN, invoice number and date, HSN/SAC, place of supply, taxable value and the CGST/SGST or IGST split. You are responsible for the details you enter.

How does Pro activation work?

After paying you get a license key by email. Paste it into BillKit (Upgrade → Activate) and Pro unlocks in that browser. You can use the same key on your other devices.
