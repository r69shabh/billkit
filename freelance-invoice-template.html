Freelance invoice template and example

A client should be able to see what they’re paying for, what they owe, and when it’s due without opening your contract. Use this example as a starting point, then replace the details with your own.

This example has no tax or discount. Sample payment note: “Pay USD 725.00 by October 16 using the agreed bank transfer details. Use INV-0042 as the reference.” On a real invoice, include both billing addresses and your payment details.

Adapt it to how you charge

  • Fixed fee: Use quantity 1 at the agreed price. Name the deliverable or milestone. For a final instalment, say it’s the balance and bill only the amount still due.
  • Hourly: Put hours in the quantity field and the hourly rate in the price field. Include the work period and attach a timesheet if your client requires one.
  • Retainer: Use one line for the agreed fee and specify the period. List approved work outside the retainer separately so it isn’t mistaken for a duplicate charge.

Before you send it

  1. Use your client’s legal billing name and the right billing contact. Include a purchase order or contract reference in Notes if their finance team requires one.
  2. Assign a unique invoice number. Enter the actual due date as well as agreed payment terms; “Net 15” means payment is due 15 days after the invoice date.
  3. Check whether tax, registration numbers or additional fields are required for your business. Add only taxes that legally apply.
  4. Choose Print / Save PDF, check the saved total, and send the PDF to the billing contact or upload it to their portal.
